Bridging The High-Stakes Delivery Gap

Technology programmes rarely fail on engineering. They fail in the gap between what the business asked for and what the delivery team was told to build — a gap that shows up later as slipped dates, grown budgets and controls nobody owns.

We sit on both sides of that gap. Ambition is translated into a costed, sequenced plan; delivery is governed against it with the evidence the board, the auditor and the regulator will ask for. The result is delivery you can commit to — on time and within budget.
